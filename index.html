AI-AC — AI Accessibility Checker Published

A published Chrome extension that scans any site and reports a score for each disability profile, not one flat list. A local rules engine (mapped to WCAG 2.2) runs for free; an optional AI vision pass reads a screenshot the way a person sees it, catching text baked into images, meaning carried by colour alone, and mismatched alt text.

04

About

I am a PhD student in Computer Science, working at the intersection of human-computer interaction and accessibility. My research asks a stubborn question: the tools we build for programming, learning, and everyday web use assume a certain kind of user, so what does it take to make them work for everyone else?

That takes me from screen-reader semantics and audio interfaces, to the lived experience of neurodivergent students, to shipping real tools like AI-AC. I move between research methods and engineering because good accessibility needs both: evidence about people, and software that actually runs.
